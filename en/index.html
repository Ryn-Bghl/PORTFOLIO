Who am I & What is my vision?

Currently studying for a BUT in Multimedia and Internet (MMI) at IUT Sénart Fontainebleau (UPEC), I operate at the intersection of rigorous web engineering and bold interaction design.

Driven by a passion for high-performance, memorable digital experiences, I craft modern, accessible, and user-centric interfaces. My workflow pairs deep technical execution (React, TypeScript, modern CSS Grid/Flexbox) with a strong aesthetic perspective drawn from architectural brutalism: intentional typography, clear visual hierarchy, and polished micro-interactions.

Through real-world and academic projects — including a complete website development for the non-profit Dons du Son and Linux remote server architectures —, I have fostered strong autonomy in component-driven development, methodical problem solving, and collaborative workflows.
